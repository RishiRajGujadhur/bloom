# Bloom: 500 quality-of-life improvements

## Research

This list was drawn from what the best everyday apps get right:
- **Linear and Raycast**: keyboard-first design, command palette everywhere, instant actions.
- **Things 3 and Todoist**: natural-language entry, quick add from anywhere, gentle undo.
- **Notion and Obsidian**: slash commands, backlinks, a page history you can trust.
- **Headspace and Calm**: one-tap start, pick up where you left off, no guilt.
- **Duolingo**: streak protection, tiny sessions, encouraging copy.
- **Apple and Google system apps**: sensible defaults, instant search, accessible by default.

Every page of Bloom was screenshotted and reviewed against those qualities. The recurring themes are:
- fewer taps to do the common thing;
- keyboard shortcuts;
- remembering state;
- undo instead of confirm;
- performance;
- deeper customisation in Settings;
- page-specific polish.

`[x]` means shipped, with the commit named in the push log at the bottom. Items are grouped by area and worked through in batches of 10, with a push after each batch.
